## 7. Key Knowledge Added

- Móc xích: each idea must bridge into the next; weak transitions break the layout.
- Tổng phân hợp: the main idea appears at the beginning and returns at the end after detailed analysis.
- Quy nạp: evidence and details come before the final conclusion.
- Liệt kê: items can stand independently but must share one topic and a deliberate order.
- Dẫn dắt thuyết phục: use quy nạp to build trust before placing the offer or CTA.
- Detailed content process: choose original layout first, then create detailed outline, then write.
- Professional layout meta-framework: original layout systems are the foundation behind ready-made formulas.
